About Us

Legacy Since 1985, Reinvented in 2013, A.P. Industries delivers innovative, reliable, and high-quality industrial packaging and heating solutions globally.

Company Overview

Reliable & Innovative Industrial Solutions

Our Journey with roots established in 1985, A.P. Industries was founded as a separate company in 2013, bringing a fresh brand identity while carrying forward decades of industry expertise and excellence.A P Industries was established in 2013 by Mr. Rajeshbhai Patel with a vision to deliver reliable, innovative, and high-quality industrial solutions. Since its inception, the company has been committed to providing advanced engineering products and exceptional customer service to industries across India and global markets.

With years of industry experience, A P Industries has earned a reputation for manufacturing durable packaging machinery, supplying premium industrial equipment, and offering dependable technical services. Our customer-focused approach, strict quality standards, and commitment to continuous improvement make us a trusted partner for businesses seeking efficient industrial solutions.

Our Core Philosophy

A P Industries is a young, dynamic, mid-sized family company producing and distributing Packaging & Heating Solutions for Global Industries.

We believe in building lasting partnerships through innovation, precision, and uncompromising quality. Our mission is to empower industries with reliable packaging and industrial heating solutions that improve productivity, optimize performance, and create long-term value for our customers. At A P Industries, our packaging and heating solutions are based on the 3P's:

Protection

Securing products against physical damage, shocks, and distribution hazards through robust and durable packaging machinery and packaging materials.

Preservation

Maintaining the structural, electrical, and thermal properties of industrial goods by utilizing precision-engineered components and heaters.

Promotion

Elevating product presentation, ease of use, and visual appeal to build trust, establish authority, and promote client brand values.
