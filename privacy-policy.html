1 Introduction & Overview

A.P. Industries ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") operates as a premier manufacturer of industrial packaging machinery, materials, industrial heaters, ovens, and control panels based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. We respect the privacy of our business clients, website visitors, and industrial partners.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your personal and corporate information when you visit our website, submit inquiry forms, request quotations, or communicate with our engineering and sales teams.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information to facilitate B2B communication, provide engineering quotations, fulfill purchase orders, and improve our services.

  • Contact & Business Information: Name, job title, company/organization name, email address, telephone/mobile number, mailing address, and GST/tax details when you fill out contact or quote request forms.
  • Inquiry & Project Details: Specific product inquiries, technical specifications, machinery requirements, furnace operating parameters, and custom heating element requests.
  • Technical Data & Device Logs: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, page views, and access timestamps collected automatically by web server infrastructure.

3 How We Use Your Information

Your information is processed strictly for legitimate industrial and commercial purposes:

  • Responding to Inquiries: Processing contact form requests, delivering technical datasheets, and sending formal commercial quotations.
  • Order Execution & Support: Manufacturing custom equipment, processing billing, arranging freight dispatch, and handling post-sale technical support or warranty services.
  • Communication Delivery: Sending transactional notifications and email responses via our secure mail delivery provider (Resend API).
  • Site Optimization: Analyzing website navigation trends to enhance our digital product catalog and user experience.
  • Compliance: Fulfilling statutory tax, accounting, and legal requirements under Indian commercial law.

4 Information Sharing & Disclosure

Our Privacy Commitment

A.P. Industries does NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal or company information to third-party advertisers or marketing brokers under any circumstances.

We may share data only with trusted partners essential to fulfilling our services:

  • Service Providers: Secure infrastructure and mail dispatch services (such as Resend API for email notifications) operating under strict confidentiality.
  • Logistics & Shipping Carriers: Freight, courier, and transport companies designated to deliver equipment and materials to your facility.
  • Legal Obligations: When disclosure is mandatory by law, court order, or authorized regulatory agencies in India.

5 Data Security Standards

We employ administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal and commercial data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

  • Encrypted Connections: All web traffic is transmitted securely using standard HTTPS (SSL/TLS) encryption protocols.
  • Access Controls: Internal access to customer data and inquiry records is restricted strictly to authorized sales, engineering, and administrative personnel.

6 Cookies & Web Technologies

Our website utilizes standard cookies and browser session storage to enhance navigation and site performance.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for proper website functionality, form submissions, and layout rendering.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us measure visitor interactions and popular product categories to improve content layout.

You can adjust your web browser settings to block or delete cookies at any time, though some website features may operate with reduced functionality.

7 Data Retention Policy

We retain personal and corporate contact records only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including satisfying ongoing B2B commercial relationships, warranty obligations, accounting audits, and statutory retention laws in India.

8 Your Rights & Data Choices

In accordance with applicable privacy regulations (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you have the right to:

  • Access & Verification: Request details of the personal contact data we hold about you or your enterprise.
  • Correction & Updates: Request rectification of inaccurate or outdated contact information.
  • Deletion: Request erasure of your contact records from our inquiry database, subject to legal and contractual retention obligations.

9 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party platforms (such as external social media pages or developer websites). A.P. Industries does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.
