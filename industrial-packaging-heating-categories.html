Packaging Machinery

At A.P. Industries, we manufacture and supply precision-engineered manual, semi-automatic, and fully automatic packaging machinery designed to enhance productivity, improve packaging quality, and ensure reliable sealing performance. Our solutions are built to meet the demanding requirements of industries such as food & beverages, pharmaceuticals, FMCG, chemicals, cosmetics, agriculture, and consumer products.

Our comprehensive range includes sealing machines, vacuum packaging machines, shrink wrapping systems, induction sealing machines, strapping machines, batch coding machines, pouch packing machines, and other secondary packaging equipment. Designed for continuous operation, our machines help reduce downtime, increase production efficiency, and deliver consistent, airtight packaging.

Manufactured using high-quality components, including food-grade SS304 and SS316 stainless steel where required, our equipment complies with industry standards for hygiene, durability, and long service life. Every machine is engineered to provide reliable performance while minimizing maintenance and operating costs.

From portable sealing and coding machines to fully automatic inline packaging systems with integrated shrink tunnels and customized pneumatic or mechanical controls, our solutions are designed to integrate seamlessly into your existing production line. We also offer customized packaging solutions tailored to your specific product, packaging material, and production requirements.

With a commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, A.P. Industries delivers packaging machinery that improves operational efficiency, ensures product safety, and supports the long-term growth of your business.

  • Engineered with a Strong MS Powder-Coated Body for Maximum Durability
  • User-friendly PLC control panels
  • Industrial-Grade Steel Body for Maximum Strength & Stability

Industrial Ovens & Furnaces

High-temperature heat treatment ovens, muffle furnaces, and batch curing chambers engineered for thermal uniformity and rapid heat cycles.

A.P Industries designs thermal curing and calcination ovens suited for drying varnishes, baking resins, heat-treating steel components, and laboratory testing. Our batch ovens operate with forced air convection cross-flow patterns, guaranteeing uniform heat distribution.

Constructed with ceramic wool linings and heavy-gauge mild steel or stainless steel casing, our ovens minimize skin temperature and save energy. Temperature curves can be programmed through digital touch-screen panels.

  • Temperature uniformity to +/- 5°C - 10°C
  • Energy-efficient ceramic insulation
